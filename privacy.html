Privacy Policy

Applies to this website (medrelief.co.in), the Med Relief Patient and Doctor apps, and the Med Relief patient and doctor portals.
Effective: 18 November 2026 · Version 1.3

1. Who we are

COLLAB CUBICLES PRIVATE LIMITED, operating as Med Relief ("Med Relief", "Medrelief", "we", "our", "us"), runs diagnostic laboratory centres in Bihar Sharif, Rajgir, Ekangarsarai and Madhubani, Bihar, India, and the Med Relief apps and website. We collect and process personal and health information to provide diagnostic testing services and to deliver reports to you. COLLAB CUBICLES PRIVATE LIMITED is responsible for your data (the data fiduciary under the DPDP Act).

This policy explains what data we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

2. Data we collect

  • Identification: full name, mobile number, age, gender, and a Medrelief patient code (e.g. MR-PAT-000123).
  • Clinical: referring doctor (if any), prescribed tests, sample type, test results, pathologist sign-off, and the lab accession number.
  • Operational: visit date, payment mode and amount, WhatsApp / SMS delivery status of your report.
  • Bookings made in the app or in the chat on this website: who the test is for, the tests, the day and time you choose, and for home collection your address and pincode. For online payments we keep the Razorpay payment reference and method (UPI, card or netbanking), never your card or bank details.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use my location" in the app or "Share my location" in the chat on this website, to find your nearest centre and check whether we can collect at home. For a home collection the location is sent with the booking so the collector can find you. Neither the app nor the website tracks your location in the background.
  • Optional: a photo of your prescription, if it is scanned so the tests can be filled in automatically. The photo is read by an AI service (see section 4) and is kept with your lab record (see section 6).
  • We do not collect Aadhaar, PAN, biometric, or financial-account information beyond the Razorpay payment confirmation reference.

3. How we use your data

  • To register your visit, generate a bill, collect samples, perform tests, generate reports, and deliver those reports to you.
  • For a home collection, the centre and the collector see your name, mobile number, address and booked tests so they can reach you.
  • To send you transactional WhatsApp and SMS messages (collection confirmation, report ready, report delivered). We do not send marketing messages.
  • To comply with our record-keeping obligations under applicable Indian laws (e.g. lab accreditation, tax).
  • To detect and prevent fraud, recover unpaid bills, and resolve customer disputes.

4. AI-assisted features

  • Several screens use AI assistance — prescription OCR, voice-to-text dictation, conversation-mode form filling, and suggested test bundles by specialty. Each of these has a manual fallback that is always one tap away.
  • Voice / speech recognition: when you or our staff use voice input, the speech recognition built into the phone or browser turns the audio into text. On Android and in Chrome the audio may be processed by Google; on iPhone and in Safari it may be processed by Apple. We never receive the audio ourselves — only the resulting text reaches our servers. Audio is not stored by Med Relief at any point.
  • Once a transcript exists, Medrelief sends only that text (no audio) to an AI service to pick out the relevant fields (your name, age, mobile, tests, etc.) so staff can fill the form faster. The receptionist confirms every extracted value before it is saved.
  • The AI service we use is Google Gemini. It is used to read prescription photos, to turn staff dictation into form fields, to read result sheets for our lab staff, and for optional report summaries that a doctor can ask for (these send only age, gender and result values, never your name or mobile number).
  • We send each AI service only what the task needs. What is hidden depends on the task: report summaries for doctors send only age, gender and result values; the billing assistant masks your mobile number. A prescription photo is sent as it was captured, with whatever is written on it (including your name), because the AI has to read it. Dictated text is sent as spoken, because the AI has to pick out your details from it. Each provider processes the data under its own API terms.
  • Every AI call is logged with a fingerprint of the request, the response, the confidence score and the cost, and is available for audit on request.

5. Where your data is stored

  • All personal and clinical data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on Amazon Web Services (Amazon RDS) in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region of India, encrypted at rest.
  • Report PDFs and prescription photos are stored in Amazon S3 in the same Mumbai region, encrypted at rest. Backups also stay in the Mumbai region.
  • AI processing happens outside India: Google Gemini processes the data on Google's servers, which may be outside India. We send it only what each task needs (see section 4).
  • WhatsApp messages we send you (report links, receipts, one-time passwords) are delivered through our provider Gupshup and pass through Meta's WhatsApp servers.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Lab reports, results and your patient record: kept while you keep visiting us and for 7 years after your last visit. After that we remove your name, mobile number and gender from your patient record and keep the record in our archive. We do not delete these records automatically.
  • Billing and payment records: 8 years from the end of the financial year, because Indian company law requires us to keep accounts that long.
  • Prescription photos: kept with your lab record under the same 7-year rule. After 90 days they move to archive storage.
  • AI interaction logs (see section 4): kept as part of our records, like your lab record, and not deleted automatically.
  • Centre-visit requests that do not become a visit: the name, mobile number, age and note are removed after 90 days.
  • Your app login: kept until you delete your account (Profile → Delete account) or ask us to. Deleting it does not delete your lab records (see section 8).

7. Who we share your data with

  • Your referring doctor, if you registered with one: they receive a copy of your report through our B2B portal.
  • Payment processors (Razorpay) and communication providers (Gupshup for WhatsApp, whose messages pass through Meta's WhatsApp service; MSG91/Fast2SMS for SMS) — only the minimum data needed to complete the transaction.
  • Speech-recognition providers (the service built into the phone or browser: Google on Android and Chrome, Apple on iPhone and Safari) — only the audio of what is said into the microphone, used to produce a text transcript. We do not receive the audio.
  • Our AI service provider (Google Gemini) — only for the tasks in section 4, with only the data each task needs. Google may process the data outside India. If we add or change an AI provider, we will update this policy first.
  • Authorities, on lawful written request (court order, statutory notice). We will inform you unless we are legally barred from doing so.
  • We do NOT sell your data or share it with advertisers.

On this website: analytics and ad measurement

On medrelief.co.in we ask before using cookies from Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel. They are used only if you tap "Allow" on the banner, to see which of our ads and posts bring people to the website and lead to a booking.

  • What is sent: pages you visit, taps on Call, WhatsApp or the booking chat, and when a booking is made or paid, its amount and our order reference.
  • What is never sent: your name, mobile number, address, age, or the tests or packages you choose.
  • Your choice: tap "No thanks" and nothing is loaded. You can change your mind at any time with "Cookie choices" at the bottom of the website.

Anonymous visit counts (no cookies): separately, the website counts visits so we know how many people our ads and posts bring. When you open the website, it sends us once per visit the page you landed on, the campaign tags in the link you used (for example "facebook" and the ad's name) and the name of the site you came from (for example instagram.com). It sets no cookie, and we do not store your IP address, a device ID, your browser details or anything else that could identify you; we only keep a running count per day. Because nothing personal is kept, this is not covered by the "Allow" banner.

8. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Right to access: ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: ask us to correct any inaccurate or out-of-date information.
  • Right to erasure: you can delete your account yourself in the app (Profile → Delete account), or ask us by email or at the counter. Deleting your account closes your app login at once and stops any doctor or company you shared your reports with from seeing them. Your lab reports and bills are not deleted, because the law requires us to keep medical and accounting records: they are kept as described in section 6, and after 7 years we remove your name, mobile number and gender from your patient record. If you ask us to erase your personal data, we will do so within 30 days, except for records the law requires us to keep.
  • Right to withdraw consent: you can withdraw your consent for any non-essential processing at any time; doing so will not affect processing already completed.
  • Right to grievance: contact our Grievance Officer (named below) if you believe we have mishandled your data.
  • To exercise any of these rights, use Profile → Delete account in the app, email privacy@medrelief.co.in, or speak to the receptionist at your centre. Identity verification is required before we release or delete data.

Without the app: see how to delete your Med Relief account (by email or at any centre).

9. Security

  • Access to your data is gated by role-based access control (RBAC). Each staff member can only access the data their role requires.
  • Access to patient records and reports is logged: who looked, when, and what.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; sessions use signed JWT tokens; PDF report links are signed S3 URLs that expire.
  • We do not guarantee that any system is breach-proof; if we do detect a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board within 72 hours.

10. Children

We register paediatric patients with the consent of a parent or guardian. The parent / guardian is treated as the data principal for the purpose of consent and exercising rights until the child turns 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any material change on this page with a new version number and effective date. Continued use of our services after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
