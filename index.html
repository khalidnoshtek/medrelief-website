How it works

Five stages. You can see all of them.

The same stages our staff work through are the ones you track against — so "where is my report" always has an answer.

STAGE 01

Registered

Book on call or walk in. You get a token and an accession number — no forms to fill twice.

STAGE 02

Billing done

Pay by UPI, card or cash — in full or part now, the rest later. Your receipt is printed and shared.

STAGE 03

Sample collected

At a center or at home, by a trained phlebotomist. Barcoded at the point of draw.

STAGE 04

In lab

Processed on connected analysers, with results feeding straight into your report.

STAGE 05

Report ready

Verified and signed by a pathologist, then sent to you on WhatsApp as a PDF.

One platform

Everything the front desk, lab and finance need.

From registration to reconciliation — a single kiosk-simple app, built mobile-first for the counter.

AI prescription scan

Reception scans the prescription; AI vision extracts the patient and the ordered tests, ready to confirm — no re-typing.

Voice assistant

Every role can complete tasks by speaking. The transcript is shown before anything is saved.

Smart billing & payments

Rate plans, discounts with approvals, coupons and packages. UPI, card or cash — including part-payments and split tender.

Sample & worklist tracking

Accessioning, barcoded specimens, collection lists and a live worklist — every sample tracked from draw to result.

Analyzer integration

Connect analyzers over HL7 and ASTM. Results match to the accession and land as a draft for the pathologist to verify.

Finance & reporting

Daily close, reconciliation, per-mode collections and bills-by-staff — the numbers your center head needs, ready to export.

How it works
