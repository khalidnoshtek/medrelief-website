Ways to work with us

Pick how you'd like to partner.

Refer patients

Doctors and clinics refer patients for a centre visit or home collection. Verified reports reach you in the Med Relief doctor app, with a statement of every referral.

Send samples to Med Relief

Labs send us the tests they don't run in-house. We process them on connected analysers and return an integrated, pathologist-signed report.

Use our collection agents

Our trained agents pick up samples from your clinic or your patients' homes and bring them to our lab — barcoded and tracked.

Become a collection agent

Collect samples on Med Relief's behalf — you don't run the tests. Get assignments and addresses in our app and hand samples over to our lab.
