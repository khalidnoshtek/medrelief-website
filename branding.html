1 · Logos

The logo files we received

Each mark is shown on our four brand backgrounds: white, plum tint, plum and ink. The plum heart fades on plum and ink, so on dark screens the mark needs a white tile (as in the website footer).

2 · App icons

Icon options for the three apps

Android launchers cut icons to a circle or squircle, and iOS uses a rounded square. Switch the shape to check that nothing important is cut off. Staff phones carry both the Staff and Patient apps, so those two icons must be easy to tell apart.
